Nuriva — Account and Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your account and associated cloud backup.

Delete in the app

Open Profile in Nuriva, select Delete Account and verify your identity.

Cannot access the app?

Send a deletion request from your registered email address. Identity verification may be required.

Request Account Deletion

What data is deleted?

In-app deletion is designed to remove your account and associated cloud backup. Worship records may remain on your device. Retention and deletion of other data require further verification.
